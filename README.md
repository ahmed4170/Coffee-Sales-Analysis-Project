# ☕ Coffee Sales Analysis Project

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes a coffee sales dataset to uncover business insights related to **sales performance, customer behavior, and product trends**. The goal is to support data-driven decision-making using Excel dashboards and structured data analysis.

---

## 📂 Dataset Description

The dataset includes the following main tables:

* **Orders**: Transaction-level data (date, quantity, price, product details)
* **Customers**: Customer demographics and loyalty status
* **Products**: Coffee types, roast types, and product categories

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze total sales performance over time
* Identify top-performing products and markets
* Understand customer purchasing behavior
* Evaluate the impact of loyalty programs

---

## 📊 Key Insights

### 1. Product Performance

* **Excelsa (Exc)** generated the highest revenue among all coffee types.
* **Liberica (Lib)** and **Arabica (Ara)** follow closely behind.
* **Robusta (Rob)** has noticeably lower sales → underperforming category.

👉 Insight: Premium or specialty coffee types are driving more revenue.

---

### 2. Geographic Sales Distribution

* **United States dominates sales** by a large margin.
* Other markets like **Ireland** and **United Kingdom** contribute significantly less.

👉 Insight: Sales are highly concentrated in one major market.

---

### 3. Customer Loyalty Behavior

* Customers **without loyalty cards generated slightly higher total revenue** than loyalty members.

👉 Insight: Loyalty program may not be effectively incentivizing higher spending.

---

### 4. Sales Trends Over Time

* Sales span from **2019 to 2022**, showing steady activity.
* Potential seasonal patterns (not fully leveraged in dashboard).

👉 Insight: Opportunity to analyze seasonality for better forecasting.

---

### 5. Order Patterns

* Some orders contain **multiple line items**, indicating bundled purchases.
* Medium roast appears to be the most frequent roast type.

👉 Insight: Customers prefer balanced flavor profiles (medium roast).

---

## 🚀 Recommendations

### 1. Optimize Product Strategy

* Focus marketing efforts on **top-performing coffee types (Excelsa, Liberica)**
* Re-evaluate **Robusta**:

  * Improve positioning OR
  * Bundle it with popular products

---

### 2. Expand International Markets

* Reduce reliance on the **U.S. market**
* Invest in:

  * Localized campaigns
  * Regional promotions in underperforming countries

---

### 3. Improve Loyalty Program

* Redesign incentives:

  * Discounts on repeat purchases
  * Points-based rewards
  * Exclusive offers for members

👉 Goal: Increase spending from loyalty customers.

---

### 4. Leverage Data for Seasonality

* Identify **peak sales months**
* Plan:

  * Promotions
  * Inventory stocking
  * Campaign launches

---

### 5. Enhance Dashboard & Reporting

* Add:

  * Time-series trends (monthly/yearly)
  * Customer segmentation visuals
  * Profit margins (not just revenue)
